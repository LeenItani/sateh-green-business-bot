Sateh Green — Rooftop Gardens for Beirut (fictional business)

Sateh Green ("sateh" means rooftop in Arabic) is a Beirut-based studio, founded in 2021, that turns unused rooftops and balconies into productive, water-wise gardens. Our mission is to make every Beirut rooftop a small source of food, shade and calm, using native Lebanese plants and irrigation that keeps working during power cuts. Our studio and showroom is in Mar Mikhael, Beirut, and we are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00. We serve Greater Beirut, the Metn and Kesrouan up to Jounieh; projects outside that area are considered case by case. To date we have completed more than 140 rooftop and balcony gardens.

We offer five services. (1) Rooftop Garden Design & Build, starting with a free site survey: the Starter Terrace package (up to 15 m2) costs $1,450, the Family Rooftop package (15 to 40 m2) costs $3,900, and the Building Commons package (over 40 m2, shared by a whole building) is quoted individually, starting from $8,500. Installation takes 2 to 3 weeks after the deposit. (2) Hydroponic Balcony Kits: the Habaq Kit, a 12-plant vertical tower, costs $289, and the Habaq Pro, a 36-plant tower with a solar-powered pump, costs $649. (3) Seasonal Care Subscriptions: Care Basic at $45 per month (one maintenance visit per month) and Care Plus at $85 per month (two visits per month plus seasonal seedling swaps). (4) Saturday Workshops at our Mar Mikhael studio for $25 per person, plus free gardening programs for public schools (two schools per month). (5) Corporate Green Roofs: consulting and installation for offices, cafes and hotels, quoted per project.

Our team is led by three co-founders. Rania Haddad (CEO) is a landscape architect who designs every garden layout. Karim Nassar (Head of Systems) is a mechanical engineer who built our solar drip-irrigation system. Maya Khoury (Head of Community & Workshops) is an agronomist who runs the workshops, school programs and plant selection.

What makes us different: every rooftop is checked for load by a licensed structural engineer before we build; our solar-powered drip irrigation keeps plants watered through power cuts; we favor native and drought-tolerant Lebanese plants such as za'atar, sumac, sage, mint and fig; our systems are greywater-ready; and our Starter Terrace comes with a Harvest Guarantee: if the garden does not produce in its first growing season, we replant it for free. Irrigation hardware carries a 12-month warranty. We accept USD cash, card and bank transfer, with a 40% deposit to start and the balance on completion.
